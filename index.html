Vanishing Emails

A system that makes expired promotional emails disappear when their purpose is over.

The idea turned invisible digital waste into a behavior brands could act on. Instead of saying less waste, the communication itself stopped wasting.

Proof: a hidden system problem turned into a simple, product-like behavior.

Method

I look for the hidden behavior inside new technology.

The tension beneath the brief. The opportunity inside the product. The human pattern hiding behind the data.

From there, I turn it into something people can use, share or act on.

It may become a campaign, a product, an interface or a new way to participate.

AI helps me explore faster. Creative judgment decides what survives.
